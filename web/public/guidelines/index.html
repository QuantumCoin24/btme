Community & Safety Guidelines

Respect and consent

Treat people respectfully. Consent must be freely given and can be withdrawn at any time.

No harassment

Threats, bullying, stalking, hate, coercion and repeated unwanted contact are not permitted.

Be authentic

Do not impersonate another person or deliberately misrepresent your identity or intentions.

No scams or exploitation

Fraud, financial exploitation, sexual exploitation and attempts to obtain money or sensitive information dishonestly are prohibited.

No minors

BTME™ is an adult dating service. Content involving sexual exploitation of minors is prohibited and may be reported to appropriate authorities.

Report concerns

Use BTME™ reporting and blocking controls where available, or contact support.
