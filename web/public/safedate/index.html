SafeDate™ is BTME™'s dedicated first-date safety layer.

Designed for real-world dating

SafeDate™ can use location-based and trusted-contact features during eligible dates, subject to the member's permissions and the state of the SafeDate session.

Guardian and trusted contacts

BTME™ may provide features that let members involve trusted contacts or use safety-assistance controls during a SafeDate™ session.

Important: SafeDate™ is a support tool and cannot guarantee personal safety. If you believe you are in immediate danger, contact the emergency services for your location.
