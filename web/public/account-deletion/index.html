Delete your BTME™ account

BTME™ provides account controls intended to allow members to manage or delete their account. Open the account/settings area of the app and follow the available account-deletion option.

If you cannot access your account, contact csscpsg.enquiries@csscpsg.co.uk.

Important: deleting your BTME™ account does not automatically cancel an active Apple subscription. Manage or cancel BTME Premium separately through your Apple account subscription settings.
